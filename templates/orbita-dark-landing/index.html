The Journey

Five days. One perfect arc around the Moon.

No docking, no station rotations. A single, quiet free-return trajectory — the safest path ever flown with civilians aboard.

D-3
Cape Meridian · Training campus

Arrival & crew rehearsal

Meet your crew and commander — a veteran of four orbital missions. Full-dress launch rehearsal, suit fitting, and your first zero-g parabola flight.

D0
Launch · 06:40 local

Ascension

Eight and a half minutes from pad to orbit. The loudest six hundred seconds of your life, followed by absolute silence as the engines cut and gravity lets go.

D2
Trans-lunar coast

Earthrise

At 07:14 UTC on day two, Earth climbs over the lunar horizon in a single slow motion. The crew goes quiet. This is the moment everyone books the flight for.

D3
Far side passage

The silence of the far side

For 48 minutes, you are more distant from every living human than anyone has ever been. Radio stays off. Dinner is served facing the terminator line.

D5
Splashdown · South Pacific

Homecoming

A daylight splashdown, a warm shower within the hour, and a private debrief over champagne. Your flight suit, mission patch and a crystal of the view — yours to keep.
